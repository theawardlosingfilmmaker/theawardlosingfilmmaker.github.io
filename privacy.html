Who we are

The 181st Degree is a film-education page on Instagram and YouTube. "The 181st Degree Publisher" is our own internal tool for posting our videos and carousels to our own accounts. Contact: the181stdegree@gmail.com.

What the publisher accesses

  • Our own YouTube channel, only to upload our own videos (the youtube.upload permission).
  • Our own Instagram account, only to publish our own posts.

The tool is not offered to the public and does not sign in, track or collect data from anyone else.

Data we store

Access tokens for our own accounts are kept encrypted in our private hosting settings and used only to publish our content. Media files are held briefly in private or temporary storage while they're being published, then deleted. We don't sell, share or analyse personal data.

Google user data

Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. You can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Visitors to this website

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It's hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs.

Changes
